# MarketForge-QTR v2.0.0 — Research-Complete v2

## Highlights

- End-to-end market-data, machine-learning, and execution-research pipeline.
- MarketForge Multi-Market Microstructure Transformer (M3T).
- Comparable supervised, SSL, EPT, and frozen-calibration ladder.
- CUDA-backed local neural training and GPU XGBoost comparison.
- Counterfactual FIFO L3 queue/fill simulation with explicit availability masks.
- Side-consistent markouts and dimensionally explicit USD execution accounting.
- One-time unseen XNAS/NVDA final lockbox with outcome-blind structural amendments.
- Isolated post-hoc revision-4 calibration, slicing, TCA, OOD, and monitoring suite.
- Scientific governance, negative-result preservation, and reproducibility controls.

## Final research finding

M3T transferred directional signal better on the unseen final domain, but XGBoost produced
better fill forecasts and neither active diagnostic generated positive after-cost economics.
The frozen `NO_TRADE` policy remained the correct decision under the v2 protocol.

The authoritative final direction macro-F1 was `0.2564` for M3T and `0.1903` for XGBoost.
Post-hoc fill Brier was `0.3032`/`0.2792` for M3T bid/ask and
`0.2592`/`0.2196` for XGBoost. Final active diagnostic net PnL was `-$6.537` for M3T and
`-$1.891` for XGBoost; no-trade returned `$0` by construction.

## Important limitations

- The central evidence contains only two development and one final instrument-day units.
- Population-level inference across instruments or markets is not established.
- Public/provider-specific observations do not represent all market microstructure.
- Execution outcomes are counterfactual `SIMULATED_L3`, not observed fills.
- Replay assumes a small order and no endogenous market impact.
- Better direction prediction did not establish a profitable after-cost trading edge.

## Data

Raw market data, final-lockbox files, row-level predictions, checkpoints, calibrators, and
controllers are **not** distributed with this release. Users must acquire compatible data
independently from official providers and comply with their current licenses and terms.

The optional release archive contains only public-safe aggregate reports, figures, tables,
and manifests. It contains no raw market data or model weights.
