# MarketForge-QTR v1 superseded notice

Effective 2026-09-27, MarketForge-QTR v1 is preserved as engineering and audit history but is scientifically superseded for economic and model-mechanism claims.

Nothing in v1 is deleted, rewritten, or relabeled as v2 evidence. In particular, `artifacts/final_evaluation_v1.json` is immutable and the v1 `data/final_lockbox` is permanently spent. Both may be used only for historical diagnosis.

## Scientific reasons

The v1 averaged bid/ask markout algebraically canceled future-price information; endpoint midpoint crossing was not a valid L3 fill label; and futures price points were mixed with USD fees. The v1 SSL-to-EPT ancestry was also not a complete factorial comparison. These defects invalidate v1 economic/model-mechanism conclusions even though raw-data, ordering, reconstruction, CUDA, systems, and some predictive evidence remain useful.

The content-addressed inventory is `artifacts/v1_superseded_manifest.json`. V2 uses new versioned labels, splits, development data, lockbox, checkpoints, freeze, evaluation, and reports.
