# Mandatory limitations

Spot FX is decentralized and public observations are provider-specific. L1 lacks exact queue/fills. FI-2010 is short and normalized. Public CME samples may have limited history. Historical replay lacks endogenous impact and simulated execution is counterfactual. One local RTX is not frontier-cluster scale. Public-data market making is not proprietary client-flow market making. Backtests do not imply future returns.
